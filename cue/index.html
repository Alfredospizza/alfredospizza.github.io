Every sentence is a cue.

Cue is a quiet player that speaks five languages. Replay what was just said, hear it in another language, or turn any sentence into a clip.

Try it

R Say that again

Missed a word? Press R or the replay button next to play. Cue replays the sentence from the pause before it. Press twice for the one before.

Aa Say it in…

After a replay, pick EN, ES, FR, DE or IT. You hear the same sentence in another voice, with subtitles to match.

X Make a clip

The seek bar turns into a range that snaps to sentences, so a clip never cuts a word. Share a link that keeps the language, or save a video with the subtitles burned in.

Read the bar

The seek bar is thick where someone speaks and a hairline where nobody does, so you can see a line coming before you hear it.

Design notes

The cue is the unit

Subtitles already cut a film into cues: one line, one thought. Most players draw them over the picture and forget them. Cue builds the controls around them. The bar shows them, the replay button repeats them, and clips snap to them.

Quiet until you reach for it

One row: play, replay, volume, time, one menu, full screen. Nothing sits on the picture. The bar fades in as the pointer comes within the bottom 30% of the player, and the subtitles step up so the bar never covers a line. Two rules guided every choice: don't make me think, and less is more.

Five languages, one asset

The English original plus Spanish, French, German and Italian voice-overs and subtitles are tracks on one Mux asset. The Video.js audio and subtitle menus fill by themselves, and the same stream works with native HLS on an iPhone.

Clips that keep the language

A link such as ?clip=35.3-47.9&audio=es&cc=es opens just that sentence, in Spanish. A saved clip carries the voice-over and burned-in subtitles, because social video is often watched muted.

Any video

Without subtitles there are no sentences, so the replay button and the speech marks simply stay away and Cue is a calm, ordinary player. Try Any plain video above.

For everyone

  • 44 px targets. Every control is the same easy size.
  • Focus you can see. A white ring with a dark halo, on bright and dark frames alike.
  • Screen readers. Clip handles are sliders with spoken times; replays, languages and clips are announced.
  • Calm on request. Reduced motion turns off every fade. Forced colours use system colours with the controls always on.
  • Keys that stay polite. Shortcuts work only when the player has focus, so they never take the page's keys (WCAG 2.1.4).
